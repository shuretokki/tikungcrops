Copernicus Sentinel data are made available free of charge, with full and open access, under the Copernicus Sentinel Data Terms.
The terms ask users to acknowledge the source, with the wording "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [year]" when the data are modified.
See https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice for the full legal notice.
